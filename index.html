The problem
"I'd have to check.""Let me get back to you.""It's in a text somewhere."

If you own a small operation, you are the filing system. The answer to almost any question is in a text thread, a spreadsheet, a PDF in Dropbox, or the head of whoever did the job.

So you chase people. You call the tech, you text the office, you wait. Not because the information does not exist, but because it is scattered across everything you run.

How it works

We claw the answers out of your scattered data. Then you ask.

1

We pull it out

We connect to the systems you already run and get the data out, including the old software nobody can export from. Where a system will not give it up, we find another way.

  • Legacy job and accounting software
  • Spreadsheets, texts, and emails
  • Signed documents and photos
2

It becomes one memory

Every asset, every customer, every job gets one record, with its history attached. New work flows in as it happens, so the memory stays current without anyone re-typing anything.

  • One record per asset, customer, and job
  • Kept current from the shop floor
  • Runs where you choose, including in your own building
3

You ask, it answers

Ask out loud or by text, from the truck or the desk, the way you would ask a person. It answers in seconds with the record behind it, so you can check it.

  • Voice or text, phone or desk
  • Answers with the source shown
  • Access controls for staff

Who it's for

Owner-operators who are the filing system.

A good fit

  • You own and run the business, with a crew of five to fifty.
  • You service, rent, lease, or maintain physical things: equipment, vehicles, trailers, properties, tools.
  • Your records live in three or more places and one of them is a person.
  • You want it handled, not another piece of software to administer.

Not a fit yet

  • You want software to install and run yourself.
  • Your data already lives in one modern system that answers your questions.
  • You are looking for a phone-answering service. This is memory, not reception.
